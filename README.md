"# Rolling Thunder" 
remake of Rolling Thunder for Amiga AGA, fastmem & whdload required

Requires a 2-button joystick, or the keyboard

Credits:

  sound conversion for the Amiga.

- no9: music
- PascalDe73: icons

Instructions:

5: insert coin
1/2: start game
arrows/joystick: move
red/ctrl: shoot
blue/alt: jump
ESC: quit current game

Cheat keys:



Story 1 (First Half)

• Area 1: Underground base entrance; maneuvering between floor levels and collecting initial pistol/machine gun ammo from doors.
• Area 2: Storage facility with multi-level shelving, crate-jumping, and grenade-dropping enemies.
• Area 3: Dark subterranean passages featuring panthers and armed Maskers.
• Area 4: Deep cavernous routes with flying Gelzos and bouncing Fire-Men.
• Area 5: Complex underground maze introducing fenced foreground and background plane transitions.

Story 2 (Second Half / Hard Mode)

• Area 6: Fenced-off facility corridors with tighter timing and ceiling laser traps.

• Area 8: High-pressure enemy squads and hazardous narrow platform jumps.
